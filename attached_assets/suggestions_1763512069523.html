Power Plants

Low Footprint:
  • Switch to LED bulbs for all lighting
  • Unplug devices when not in use
  • Use natural light during the day
  • Install a smart thermostat
Medium Footprint:
  • Consider solar panels for your home
  • Upgrade to energy-efficient appliances
  • Reduce AC/heating usage by 2-3°C
  • Use power strips to eliminate phantom loads
High Footprint:
  • URGENT: Switch to renewable energy provider
  • Replace old appliances immediately
  • Install solar panels or join community solar
  • Conduct a home energy audit

Transport

Low Footprint:
  • Great! Keep walking/biking
  • Share your eco-friendly commute tips
  • Consider carpooling occasionally
  • Maintain your bike regularly
Medium Footprint:
  • Walk or bike for trips under 2 km
  • Use public transit 2-3 times per week
  • Carpool when possible
  • Consider a hybrid or electric vehicle
High Footprint:
  • URGENT: Reduce car usage by 50%
  • Switch to public transit immediately
  • Work from home when possible
  • Trade in for a fuel-efficient vehicle

Industries

🌱 Low Impact Consumption:
  • Excellent! Continue buying locally-sourced items
  • Choose products with minimal packaging
  • Support sustainable and ethical brands
  • Share your eco-conscious shopping habits
🏭 Medium Impact Consumption:
  • Buy from local producers when possible
  • Choose products with eco-certifications
  • Reduce consumption of heavily-processed goods
  • Consider buying second-hand items
🏢 High Impact Consumption:
  • URGENT: Reduce overall consumption by 30%
  • Switch to sustainable and local alternatives
  • Avoid fast fashion and disposable products
  • Choose plant-based foods and eco-friendly goods

Waste Management

Low Footprint:
  • Perfect! Keep recycling and composting
  • Share zero-waste tips with friends
  • Use reusable bags and containers
  • Repair items instead of replacing
Medium Footprint:
  • Start composting organic waste
  • Recycle properly (clean & sorted)
  • Use reusable water bottles
  • Avoid single-use plastics
High Footprint:
  • URGENT: Start recycling immediately
  • Set up home composting system
  • Eliminate single-use plastics
  • Buy products with minimal packaging
